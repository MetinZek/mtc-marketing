## Principles

1. **Clarity first.** Short, plain copy; one idea per section; the hero says
   what MTC is and what to do next within the first screen.
2. **Colour owns regions.** White and frost grounds; colour lives in the
   cube's light, the four discipline faces and the real work.
3. **The work is the proof.** Real projects early, large and moving. No
   invented clients, quotes, numbers or awards.
4. **One memorable object.** The ice cube appears twice: as the hero, and as
   the turning centrepiece for the four disciplines.
5. **Motion with a job.** Every animation explains a change (enter, turn,
   wipe). Transform and opacity only. Reduced motion keeps every word and
   drops the movement.
6. **Built for phones.** Separate mobile compositions, 44px tap targets, and
   the CTA visible on the first screen.

## Visual system

- **Type:** Archivo (variable, width 62-125). Display set expanded
  (width 112-118) and tight; body at normal width. One family.
- **Colour:** canvas `#FFFFFF`, frost `#F4F7FD`, ink `#0B1222`, muted
  `#4A5468`, brand blue `#0B45FF` (from the logo gradient), sky `#22A0FF`.
  Discipline hues: amber `#FFB020`, blue `#2563FF`, coral `#FF5A5F`,
  mint `#14B87C`.
- **Shape:** radius 12 / 20 / 28px, pill buttons and chips; hairlines in
  cool grey; shadows tinted to the ink hue with offset and blur.
- **Ice detail:** cards and buttons catch a moving specular glint on hover,
  and the cube is the one place where glass and refraction are used.
